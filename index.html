About

Science for cleaner water

I am an environmental scientist, researcher and environmental health professional with an interdisciplinary background in microbiology, environmental resources management and environmental science. I hold a B.Sc. in Microbiology and an M.Sc. in Environmental Resources Management from Lagos State University, Nigeria, and I am completing a Ph.D. in Environmental Science at Georgia Southern University (Spring 2027).

My doctoral research designs and evaluates sustainable functional materials for removing hazardous and emerging contaminants from water, particularly natural deep eutectic solvent (NADES) modified alginate composites and functionalized ionic-liquid-based materials (GUMBOS). The work centres on response surface methodology, adsorption kinetics, equilibrium modeling and analytical characterization.

Beyond research, I bring field experience in environmental health, environmental supervision, waste management, laboratory safety and undergraduate teaching across Nigeria and the United States.

Professional goal

To apply my research expertise and analytical problem-solving to advance sustainable water management, environmental protection and public health in industry, consulting, government or research institutions.
